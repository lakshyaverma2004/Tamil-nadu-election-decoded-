### **Slide 2: Turnout & Votes Comparison (The Turnout Illusion)**
*   **Title:** Turnout & Votes Comparison (2021 vs. 2026)
*   **Visual Type:** Parallel comparative cards comparing 2021 vs. 2026 ECI metrics:
    *   *2021:* Electorate: 6.29 Cr | Votes: 4.63 Cr | Turnout: 73.38%
    *   *2026:* Electorate: 5.73 Cr | Votes: 4.83 Cr | Turnout: 84.69%
*   **Core Headline/Takeaway:** Turnout percentage jumped by **11.31%**, but actual votes polled grew by **only 20 Lakh**. More people voted, but the percentage jump is primarily due to a smaller voter list base.

### **Slide 3: Voter Base & SIR Impact (The Base-Change Effect)**
*   **Title:** ECI Special Intensive Revision (SIR) Impact
*   **Visual Type:** Proportional flow card showing ECI's list revision:
    *   *Voters Removed (SIR):* 56 Lakh
    *   *New Voters Added:* 10 Lakh
    *   *Net Electorate Reduction:* 46 Lakh (from 6.29 Cr to 5.73 Cr)
*   **Core Headline/Takeaway:** ECI's Special Intensive Revision (SIR) net reduction of 46 Lakh voters is the structural reason behind the inflated turnout percentage (+11.31%), proving that "Record Turnout != Record Wave."
